# Hospital / clinic outreach scripts

Personalize the `[brackets]`. Max 4–6 lines per first message. Always offer a **live demo**, not a brochure dump.

## Ideal customer profile (ICP)

| Segment | Triggers | Lead with |
|---|---|---|
| Nursing homes / 20–100 bed hospitals | Excel billing, pharmacy mismatch | HospitalOS |
| Multi-specialty day hospitals | OPD + IPD chaos | HospitalOS |
| Dental / polyclinic chains | No-shows, multi-doctor diary | CareDesk |
| Single clinic wanting online booking | Phone-only appointments | CareDesk |
| Diagnostic centres | Multi-test billing | HospitalOS (lab/billing) |

## Where to find them (priority order)

1. **Google Maps** — “hospital near [city]”, “dental clinic [area]” → WhatsApp / call  
2. **IndiaMART** — catalogue: Hospital Management Software, Clinic Management Software  
3. **Justdial / Sulekha** — claim + respond fast  
4. **LinkedIn** — Hospital Administrator, Medical Director, Clinic Owner, Dental Chain Founder  
5. **Facebook groups** — hospital admin / clinic owners (value first)  
6. **Partners** — CAs who serve doctors, hospital interior vendors, biomedical equipment sellers (10–20% referral)

Daily mix suggestion: 20 Maps/WhatsApp · 15 LinkedIn · 10 IndiaMART replies · 5 partner touches.

## Objection cheat sheet

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| “Send brochure” | “I’ll send a 1-page + live demo link — brochure won’t show billing flow. 10-min screen share better?” |
| “We already have software” | “What frustrates you most today — billing, IPD, or appointments? If nothing, I won’t push.” |
| “Call next year” | “Noted — I’ll diary `[month]`. Meanwhile demo link stays live if staff want a peek.” |
| “Cheapest quote wins” | “We can phase Phase-1 OPD+billing to fit budget; full HMS later.” |
| “Is source code ours?” | “License options in proposal — data is always yours; framework licensing we clarify in writing.” |

## Compliance in outreach

- Do not claim “government approved HMS” unless you have certification.  
- Do not use another hospital’s real patient screenshots.  
- Do not market as a substitute for clinical judgment or insurance licensing.
