# Restaurant outreach scripts (FoodKing)

## Where to find leads
1. Google Maps: `restaurant [area]`, `cloud kitchen`, `café [city]`  
2. Zomato/Swiggy listed outlets without own ordering app  
3. IndiaMART: “restaurant management software”, “online food ordering website”  
4. Local F&B Facebook groups  
5. Walk-in / visiting cards from your city (photo → CRM)

> Live demo: https://nexteradigitaltech.in/demos/foodking/
