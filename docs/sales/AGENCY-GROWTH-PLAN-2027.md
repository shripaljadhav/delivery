### Why not OTT / taxi as lead (despite your instinct)

**OTT**
- Your past clients shut down under **regulatory / ops reality** — buyers in this niche are often undercapitalised.
- Content licensing, CDN cost, payment disputes, compliance — **your support load explodes**.
- LiveStage is **not a working live demo** today.
- Harder to get recurring AMC from failing streamers.

**Taxi**
- Two-sided marketplace: drivers + riders. Software is 30% of the problem; **liquidity and ops are 70%**.
- Maps/SMS/payment burn cash; clients blame the agency when the city doesn’t adopt the app.
- Crowded “Uber clone” market; trademark-sensitive marketing.
- No live taxi demo on site.

**When to reopen OTT/taxi:** After ₹25L+ cumulative collections, 1–2 delivery engineers, and a hardened demo + ops playbook. Not month 1–6.

### Gym — your forgotten product

- **Market is real:** India gyms still on Excel/WhatsApp; branded member app is a strong pitch.
- **Problem:** Cheap SaaS exists at **₹500–₹3,000/month**. Owners compare you to Okfit/GymForce, not to custom agencies.
- **Your winning angle:** White-label **web + Android/iOS under their brand** + biometric/UPI + multi-branch — priced **₹1.5L–₹4.5L + AMC**, not ₹15k lifetime.
- **Do not pitch gym until** a live demo URL + APK exists on the site. Until then it is vapour.

## 8. AI agents — what to automate vs what needs humans

### Rely on AI heavily (with your review)

| Workflow | Agent use | Human check |
|---|---|---|
| Lead research (Maps lists, LinkedIn snippets) | High | Spot-check 10% |
| First-draft WhatsApp / email / LinkedIn | High | You send / personalize 1 line |
| Proposal / SoW draft from template | High | You approve price & scope |
| Feature comparison / discovery notes | High | You confirm on call |
| Meeting summaries → CRM fields | High | You set next action |
| Ad creative variants / captions | Medium | Marketer approves |
| Monthly report narrative | Medium | Numbers from real ads manager |
| Code helpers / bug triage | Medium | Engineer merges |

### Do **not** fully trust AI alone

| Workflow | Why human required |
|---|---|
| **Price negotiation & discounting** | Margin death |
| **Scope promises on call** | Delivery risk |
| **Healthcare / school compliance claims** | Legal |
| **Live product demo to buyer** | Trust; AI screen-share fails socially |
| **Ad account strategy & budget scaling** | Real money burn |
| **Production deploy / patient data** | Irreversible harm |
| **Hiring / firing / partner deals** | Judgment |

### When you need a human salesperson / “counselor”

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| You cannot answer WhatsApp &lt;2h | Hire VA/SDR |
| &gt;15 qualified demos/month | Hire BDE to book; you close |
| Deals &gt;₹5L or multi-branch hospital | You + optional senior advisor on call |
| Marketing retainers &gt;8 | Human media buyer (not AI-only) |
| AMC clients &gt;15 | Support human |

**AI is your force multiplier from day 1. A human closer is required the moment a buyer says “let’s discuss price” on a serious deal — that person can be you until Phase 2.**

Suggested agent stack (practical):
1. **Research agent** — build clinic/hospital lists by city  
2. **Outbound agent** — draft sequences in your voice  
3. **Proposal agent** — fill templates from call notes  
4. **Support agent** — draft replies to AMC tickets for your approval  
5. **Ads creative agent** — variants (human schedules)
